LineFit user guide

Everything LineFit can do, in plain words. LineFit shows whether each CV point fits on one Superset line and helps you fix the ones that don't, by hand or with AI.

Quick start

2 minutes
  1. Pick your section at the top: Experience & internship, PoR · Projects · ECA · CCA, or Spikes ribbon.
  2. Paste your points in the left box, one point per line. Bold words the way they are in your CV.
  3. Read the colour of each result: green fits, amber is borderline, red is too long.
  4. Fix it by editing the text, or click the AI button on a point to rewrite it.
  5. Click Copy text and paste it into Superset. The bold comes with it.

"pt" (points) is the unit Superset's PDF uses for width. You don't need to know it: the colours tell you what to do.

2. Typing and pasting points

  • One point per line. Press Enter to start a new point. Each line gets its own result card.
  • Bold: select words and press Ctrl + B (⌘ + B on a Mac), or click the B Bold button. Do the same again to unbold. Bold text is wider, so bold your CV exactly as it will appear.
  • Pasting keeps bold from Superset, Word, Google Docs and AI chats. Text marked with **double asterisks** turns into bold automatically.
  • Small things are handled for you: a bullet at the start (•, - or *) is ignored, and repeated spaces count as one, just like Superset.
  • Examples loads three sample points: one short, one that fits, one too long. Clear empties the box.
  • Your text is saved in your browser, so it's still there when you come back on the same device.

3. Reading the results

Each point gets a card on the right. It updates as you type.

Fits (green)

Sits on one line. "Room" tells you how much more you could add, in pt and roughly in characters.

Borderline (amber)

Right at the edge. It often fits, but check it once in Superset, or trim a character to make it green.

Too long (red)

It will wrap onto a second line. The card says how much to cut, in pt and roughly in characters.

The preview

Your point is drawn at its real width in the CV font. The dashed line is the end of the line; any words past it turn red.

The bar

Green shows how full the line is, amber is the borderline zone, and red is how far the point runs past the line.

The numbers

Width in pt and as a % of the line. Around 98 to 99% is a nicely full line.

The summary

Above the cards: how many points fit, are borderline or are too long.

4. Checking in Superset, and teaching LineFit your CV

Column widths can differ slightly between CVs, so amber and red cards ask "Checked it in Superset?" After you try the point in Superset:

  • It fit on one line tells LineFit your CV has a bit more room. Its limits move up to match.
  • It wrapped tells LineFit your line is shorter. Its limits move down.
  • Once you've used these, the top of the results says "your limits". You can also type limits yourself, or go back to the defaults, under How the measurement works at the bottom of the page.

5. Copying into Superset

Click Copy text on a card, then paste into the matching Superset field. The bold comes with it. If your browser or Superset drops the bold, re-bold those words in Superset.

6. AI rewrite

Optional

Each card has an AI button. The main part does the suggested action for that point; the ▾ next to it shows all four options.

Enlarge

Makes a short point longer so it fills the line, without adding new facts.

Shrink

Makes a long point shorter so it fits, without dropping numbers or key facts.

Improve wording

Stronger verb, clearer action → method → result, same facts, same length.

Alternatives

The same facts told from different angles, for example leading with the result or the method.

In the AI window

  1. Check the mode at the top. You can switch between the four at any time.
  2. Optional: add facts in "Facts the AI may add", such as team size, tool or time frame. The AI uses only these and what's already in your point.
  3. Automatic tab (needs a key, see Setting up AI): click Write options. LineFit asks the AI for 5 versions, measures each one, and tries up to 3 rounds until some land on target. Stop cancels.
  4. Copy-paste tab (no key needed): click Copy prompt, paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or any AI chat, then paste the AI's whole answer back into the box. Every version is measured instantly. If none fit, Copy follow-up prompt tells the AI exactly how far off each one was.
  5. Pick a result. Options marked On target fill the line without going over. Click Use this to replace your point, or Copy to copy it.
  • Undo rewrite (under your points) brings back your original text.
  • New-number warning: if an option contains a number that isn't in your point or your facts, it's flagged. Only use it if the number is true.
  • Target width: the AI aims just under the amber zone: about 98.3 to 99.5% of the line for Experience & internship, and 98 to 99% for PoR · Projects · ECA · CCA.
  • Always read the result. AI can make mistakes. Every word on your CV should be something you can explain in an interview.

7. Setting up AI

  1. Get a free key. The 2-minute key guide shows exactly how. Google Gemini is the easiest. Use your personal Gmail, not your college email: college accounts often can't create Gemini keys.
  2. Click Set up AI at the top right, choose your provider and paste the key.
  3. Click Test key. "Key works" means you're ready, and the top button changes to show your AI.
  • Remember my key on this device keeps the key in your browser so you don't paste it again. Leave it off on shared computers.
  • Follow the CV guides' rules (on by default) adds the main rules from the FMS CV guides to every AI request: strong past-tense verbs, action → method → result, no invented or rounded-up numbers, plain English.
  • Model is picked for you. Change it only if you know which one you want; Test key lists the ones your key can use.

8. Spikes ribbon

  • One spike per box, the way you enter them in Superset. Each box shows its width. Add a spike adds a box, and ✕ removes one.
  • The preview shows your ribbon at real size: dark bar, white bold text and dividers. Text that runs off the ends turns red.
  • The whole ribbon is bold, and every space counts (unlike the points, where repeated spaces count once).
  • Spread evenly with spaces adds the same number of spaces beside every divider to fill spare room neatly. Copy next to a spike then copies it with those spaces.
  • Tip from the CV guides: the 1st, 3rd and 5th spikes catch the eye most, so put your strongest ones there.

9. CV writing guides

Open Help & guides at the top right to read the two FMS guides as PDFs: The CV Prompt Bus (ready-made prompts for writing and checking points with any AI) and Fantastic CVs and How to Build Them (the rules for strong, defensible points). The same menu links to the AI key guide and this user guide.

10. Your data, and other settings

  • Your points stay in your browser. LineFit has no server and stores nothing. When you use AI, the point goes straight from your browser to the AI provider you chose.
  • Light or dark mode: the page follows your device. Use the button at the top right to switch, and it remembers your choice.
  • Tips strip: close it with ✕; it stays hidden on that device.

Disclaimer: widths are calculated, not taken from Superset. They are estimates for the Superset CV template (Calibri 10 pt), and Superset's final layout can differ, so check amber points once in Superset before you finalise. LineFit is not affiliated with Superset or any AI provider.

11. Questions

A point shows green but wrapped in Superset.
Click It wrapped on an amber card next time, or lower "Fits up to" under How the measurement works. LineFit will then match your CV.
Why does bolding a word change the width?
Bold letters are wider. Bold the same words as in your CV so the measurement matches.
I can't create a Gemini key.
You're probably using your college email. Switch to your personal Gmail on the key page and try again.
The AI button says I need a key.
Use the Copy-paste tab, which works with any AI chat, or set up a free key with the key guide.
The AI's options are all too short or too long.
Click Write more options, or add real facts in "Facts the AI may add" so it has more to work with when enlarging.
Can I use LineFit on my phone?
Yes. Everything works on a phone; editing is easiest on a laptop.
