Superset Line Fit

Check whether each CV point fits on one Superset line before you upload it. Points are measured as you type, in Calibri 10 pt.

Bold: select text, Ctrl/⌘+B
  1. Pick the section from the drop-down: work experience and internship, or PoR, projects, ECA and CCA.
  2. Paste your points, one per line. Bold words the way they are in your CV: select them and press Ctrl/⌘+B.
  3. Read the bar. Green fits. Amber is borderline and may wrap. Red is too long.
  4. Copy text and paste into Superset. The bold comes with it.

How the measurement works
  • Text is measured in Carlito, which has the same character widths as Calibri, at 10 pt with bold runs measured in bold.
  • Superset only tightens the spacing where a letter meets punctuation or a slash (as in “y,” “P.” or “V/”), never between two letters, so the tool does the same. Calibrated on 21 Superset CVs: 747 bullet lines and 55,000 letter pairs.
  • Pasted text keeps its bold from Superset, Word, Google Docs or Claude; **double asterisks** in pasted text also turn into bold.
  • Repeated spaces count once and a leading bullet (•, -, *) is ignored, as Superset does.
  • The bullet column is not exactly the same width in every CV: across the 21 CVs it varies by about 1 pt. So the limits are ranges, not one number.
  • Fits means it fits even in the narrowest column seen (work experience and internship: 470.7 pt; PoR, projects, ECA and CCA: 434.6 pt). Too long means it is wider than any point that has fitted in a column like yours (471.8 pt and 436.5 pt). In between is Borderline: it fits in most CVs, but check it once in Superset.
  • When you test a Borderline point in Superset, click It fit or It wrapped on it. The tool moves its limits to match your own CV. You can also type limits below.
  • "Characters" are estimates based on the average letter width of that point. The pt figures are measured.

Disclaimer: Widths are calculated, not taken from Superset. They are calibrated against 21 Superset CVs (FMS template, Calibri 10 pt) and are usually within a fraction of a point, but Superset's final layout can differ.
