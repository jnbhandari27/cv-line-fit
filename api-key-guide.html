Google Gemini

Free · Recommended

Use your personal Gmail, not your college email. College accounts (like @fms.edu) are managed by the college, and Google AI Studio is often switched off for them, so the key page may not open or the key may not work. Sign in with your personal Google account instead.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your personal Gmail. If you're already signed in with your college email, switch accounts first (click your profile picture at the top right).
  2. If Google shows terms of service, read and accept them.
  3. Click Create API key. If it asks for a project, pick the one it suggests or create a new one.
  4. Click Copy. Your key is a long code that starts with AIza.
  5. In LineFit, follow Add the key to LineFit and choose Google Gemini.
Good
  • Free, no card
  • Uses the Google account you already have
  • Strong, careful writing
Watch out
  • On the free tier, Google may use your text to improve its products
  • Daily cap; resets every day

Groq

Free · Fast
  1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign in. Google, GitHub or email all work; a personal email is safest.
  2. Click Create API Key and give it any name, for example LineFit.
  3. Copy the key straight away. It starts with gsk_ and Groq shows it only once.
  4. In LineFit, choose Groq and paste the key.
Good
  • Free, no card
  • Very fast answers
  • Doesn't keep your prompts by default
Watch out
  • Runs open models such as Llama, which are a little less polished than Gemini
  • Free models can change without notice

OpenRouter

Free models
  1. Open openrouter.ai/keys and sign in.
  2. Click Create key, name it, and copy it. It starts with sk-or-.
  3. In LineFit, choose OpenRouter, paste the key and click Test key. LineFit picks a free model for you (free models end in :free).
Good
  • One key for many different models
  • Free models cost nothing
Watch out
  • Only about 50 free requests a day
  • Some free models are run by companies that may log or train on your prompts. Check the privacy settings in your OpenRouter account.

OpenAI (ChatGPT's maker)

Paid

Note: a ChatGPT Plus subscription does not include API use. The API is billed separately, so you need to add a little prepaid credit.

  1. Open platform.openai.com, sign in, and add a small prepaid credit under Billing.
  2. Go to API keys and click Create new secret key.
  3. Copy the key. It starts with sk-, and you won't see it again.
  4. In LineFit, choose OpenAI and paste the key.
Good
  • Very reliable
  • API data isn't used for training by default
  • A rewrite costs a fraction of a cent
Watch out
  • Needs a card and prepaid credit
  • Set a low spending limit in Billing to be safe

Anthropic Claude

Paid

Add the key to LineFit

  1. Open LineFit and click Set up AI at the top right.
  2. Choose your provider, for example Google Gemini.
  3. Paste your key and click Test key. A green "Key works" message means you're done.
  4. Optional: tick Remember my key on this device so you don't have to paste it again. Leave it unticked on a shared or college computer.
  5. Click Enlarge, Shrink or Improve on any point, then Write options.

If something goes wrong

The Gemini key page won't open, says "not available", or the key doesn't work
You're probably signed in with your college email. Sign out, or switch to your personal Gmail, and create the key again.
"The provider rejected this key"
Copy the key again, without spaces, and paste it. If it still fails, create a new key.
"The provider's free limit was reached"
Wait a minute and try again, or switch to another free provider (for example Groq) in Set up AI.
"That model is not available on this key"
Click Test key; LineFit picks a model that works.
"Couldn't reach the provider"
Check your internet, turn off any VPN or ad blocker for this site, or try another provider.

Keep your key safe

  • Treat your key like a password. Never share it or post it anywhere.
  • LineFit never stores your key on its site. It stays in your own browser, and only if you tick "Remember my key".
  • Your points go straight from your browser to the provider you choose. Don't send anything confidential, such as client names under NDA.
  • If a key leaks, delete it on the provider's key page and create a new one.
